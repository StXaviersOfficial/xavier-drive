XavierDrive — Privacy Policy

XavierDrive is the official portal of St. Xavier's Jr./Sr. School, Muzaffarpur. This policy explains what we handle.

Account data

We store your Google account email, display name and profile picture, plus your role and class, to operate the portal. This data comes from Google Sign-In.

Your content

Files you upload, chats with the AI assistant and their titles, and settings are stored to provide the service. Chats and files can be deleted from within the app.

AI assistant

Messages you send may be processed by AI providers to generate answers, and may be searched on the public web when the assistant decides research is needed. Do not share secrets or sensitive personal information in chat.

Cookies

A session cookie keeps you signed in. It is removed when you log out.

Contact

Questions about privacy can be raised with the school administration.
