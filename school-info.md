## Academics

- **Streams (Class 11–12):** PCM (Physics, Chemistry, Maths, optional CS),
  PCB (Physics, Chemistry, Biology, optional Maths), Commerce (Accountancy,
  Business Studies, Economics, Maths), Arts (History, Political Science,
  Geography, Psychology).
- **AISSCE 2026:** 100% result. School topper Krishna Saraf scored 97.2%.
- **Timetable:** 8 periods/day, Mon–Sat, assembly 08:00, day ends ~15:00.
- **Highlights:** smart classes, science/computer/language labs, swimming
  pool, sports academy (basketball, yoga, aerobics, indoor games),
  auditorium, music & dance rooms, kindergarten wing, CCTV security,
  on-campus medical room, school transport fleet.

## Admissions (Session 2026–27)

- Open for Nursery to Class 11 (rolling admissions).
- Process: Registration (₹1,500) → interaction/assessment → offer &
  confirmation → fee payment & joining.
- Age criteria: Nursery 3+, LKG 4+, UKG 5+, Class 1 6+ (as on 31 March).
- Documents: birth certificate, 4 passport photos, Aadhaar (student &
  parents), TC (Class 2+), last report card.
